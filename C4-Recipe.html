Indian-style Tea (Chai)



Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons tea leaves (or 1 tea bag)
  • 2 teaspoons sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2–3 crushed cardamom pods (optional for flavor)
  • 1 small piece of ginger, crushed (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, add 1 cup water and bring it to a boil.
  2. Add the tea leaves (or tea bag), along with optional ginger and cardamom.
  3. Let it simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add milk and sugar, then stir well.
  5. Boil the tea for another 2–3 minutes until it reaches your desired strength and color.
  6. Strain the tea into a cup and serve hot.

Enjoy your homemade chai!
